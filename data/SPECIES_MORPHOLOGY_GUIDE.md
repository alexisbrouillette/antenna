# 🦋 Entomological Species Morphology & Trait Guide

This guide documents the verbatim morphological descriptions, anatomical notation keys, and plate illustration references extracted from historical taxonomic monographs.

## 📌 Anatomical Notation Glossary
* **Vein Numbers (1–12, M, Cu, R):** Standard Comstock-Needham wing venation system. Veins are numbered from trailing edge to leading edge.
* **Tibial Spurs:** Articulated sensory/defensive spines on legs (e.g. 'four spurs' = 2 apical + 2 median).
* **Expanse / Vlugt:** Wingspan measured tip-to-tip across outstretched forewings.
* **Tab. / Plate (e.g. Tab. XV. fig. 13):** References to original full-color hand-painted lithographic plates in the accompanying monograph atlas.
